THE STERLINGS / WEBSITE INFORMATION

How this website
handles information.

This page describes the data-handling behavior of the current Sterlings website, its browser-based Studio, and the email inquiry process.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

ON THIS PAGE

Email inquiriesStudio draftsWebsite estimatesExternal servicesAccounts and paymentsQuestions

1. Email inquiries

When you complete the inquiry form and select Prepare my inquiry email, this website prepares an email in your device's email application. It does not itself send or store that inquiry on our server.

You choose whether to send the message. If you send it, the details you enter (such as your name, email address and project description) are delivered to the recipient mailbox and handled through the relevant email providers. Please avoid including passwords, banking information or other sensitive material in an inquiry.

2. Website Studio drafts

The current Studio stores drafts using your browser's local storage so the editor can restore your work on the same device and browser. Drafts are not synced to an online Sterlings account. You may reset your draft in the Studio or clear your browser's local website data.

When you export your website, the Studio generates an HTML file for you to download. Exporting is not publishing or hosting your website.

Website estimates

The estimator calculates in your browser. Choices are stored on this device only when you select Save choices on this device; you can remove that saved draft with Start again. Names, email addresses and project notes are not included in the saved choices.

Shared estimate links contain the selected website requirements, not contact details, project notes or fixed prices. Anyone with the link can view those requirements. Prices are recalculated using the current rate card. Downloaded summaries contain your selections; printed summaries also include any project details you entered. Preparing a quotation email does not send it. You choose whether to send from your own email app or copy the request elsewhere.

3. External services

The website is delivered through a hosting provider. Technical requests to the website may be handled or logged by that provider under its own policies. The site also uses externally supplied fonts and illustrative photographs, which may cause requests to providers such as Google Fonts and Unsplash.

The current frontend does not include a custom advertising pixel or a site-specific marketing analytics script. Third-party providers may process technical information when their resources are requested.

4. Accounts, orders and payments

This public website does not currently provide live customer accounts, automated rental subscriptions or an online checkout. Website rental, managed development and custom digital solutions are discussed through inquiry and quotation before any service agreement.

Where a future service requires different information handling, its relevant notices and agreement terms should be provided before it becomes available.
